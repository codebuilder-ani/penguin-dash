# 🐧 Penguin Dash

Hi! I'm **Anirudh**, I'm 9 years old, and I made this game.

It's a running game. You are a penguin on the ice in Antarctica. You run
and run and never stop. Try to get as far as you can! 🧊

## 🎮 Play it here

### 👉 [codebuilder-ani.github.io/penguin-dash](https://codebuilder-ani.github.io/penguin-dash/)

It works on a computer and on a phone. 📱💻

## 🕹️ How to play

On a computer:

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `W` or `↑` | 🦘 Jump |
| `S` or `↓` | 🛷 Duck (hold it down) |
| `P` | ⏸️ Pause |
| `M` | 🔇 Mute |

On a phone: **tap the top** of the screen to jump, and **tap and hold
the bottom** to duck.

## 🎯 The rules

- 🧊 **Icebergs** — jump over them
- ⚙️ **Sawblades** — duck under them (you can't jump over these ones!)
- ❄️ **Snowflakes** — grab them, each one is 10 points
- 💥 If you hit anything, the run is over

You only get points from snowflakes. Just running doesn't score. 😄

## ⚡ Power-ups

- 🛡️ **Shield** — saves you one time
- 🧲 **Magnet** — pulls snowflakes to you
- ❄️ **Freeze** — makes everything slow down

## 🎵 Music

There are two songs, **Snow Day** and **Ice Slide**. Press the ♪ button
at the top to pick one. The cool part is the music isn't a file — the
computer makes it up out of beeps while you play! 🤖

## 🛠️ How I made it

It's all HTML and JavaScript in one file. No downloads, no installing
anything. Just open it and play. 🚀
