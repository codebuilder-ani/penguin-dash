On a phone or tablet: tap the top of the screen to jump, tap and hold the bottom to duck.

  • Jump over icebergs. Duck under sawblades.
  • Every snowflake is worth 10 points. Running is free — only flakes score.
  • One hit ends the run, so watch the sky as well as the ice.
  • 🛡 Shield blocks one crash. 🧲 Magnet pulls snowflakes in. ❄ Freeze slows the obstacles down.
